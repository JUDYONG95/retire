How this works: contributions are added monthly and grow each year by the increase you set. Returns compound monthly. From retirement, spending is withdrawn monthly and rises with inflation; other income offsets it from the age it starts. "Today's money" divides future amounts by cumulative inflation so you can compare them with prices now.

This is a planning estimate, not financial advice. Real returns vary year to year, and a run of bad years early in retirement can shorten the runway.
